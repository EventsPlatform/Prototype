How Lensly works
Step 1
Browse & compare

Filter by service type, city and price. Ratings only appear once a provider has 10+ reviews, so early scores can't skew your decision.

Step 2
Book & pay securely

Pay a deposit to confirm, with optional shoot insurance. VAT and platform fees are itemized up front — nothing hidden.

Step 3
Receive your work

Once you confirm delivery, funds are released to the provider. Leave a review to help the next customer.
